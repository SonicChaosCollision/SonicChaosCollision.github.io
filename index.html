MAIN PLAYABLE CHARACTERS

SONIC

Sonic is a blue hedgehog who lives on planet Earth. He is always running all over the world at supersonic speeds, carefree and relaxed, but will never tolerate injustice.

TAILS

Miles, better known by his nickname "Tails", is Sonic's faithful sidekick. He uses his two tails to fly like a helicopter and has a brilliant knack for machinery.

KNUCKLES

Knuckles the Echidna is the hot-tempered inhabitant of Angel Island and the last guardian of the Master Emerald, defining his fighting style with fierce fists.

SHADOW

Shadow is the "ultimate life form", created in an experiment aboard the Space Colony ARK. He utilizes Chaos Control to slice through dimensional enemies.

SPONGEBOB

Bikini Bottom's favorite fry cook, armed with bubble-spinning powers, karate chops, and endless optimism to protect his friends across dimensions.

PATRICK

SpongeBob's loyal best friend who brings heavy-hitting, unpredictable star power and surprising physical durability to the team.

SQUIDWARD

Reluctant hero armed with sonic clarinet blasts and long-range tentacle attacks, fighting just so he can get back to his quiet life.

LARRY

Goo Lagoon's muscular lifeguard who delivers raw physical strength, high defense, and crushing claw attacks to clear paths.

BLOSSOM

The strategic leader of the Powerpuff Girls, featuring ice breath, tactical flight, and brilliant quick-thinking leadership in combat.

BUBBLES

The sweet powerhouse whose supersonic screams, animal communication, and destructive energy blasts pack a massive punch.

BUTTERCUP

The toughest fighter of the trio, charging headfirst into battle with relentless brawling skills, heavy hits, and raw aggression.

UNLOCKABLE CHARACTERS

SANDY

Texas karate master and scientist equipped with high-flying kicks, lasso maneuvers, and inventive tech gadgets for stage traversal.

AMY

Cheerful hedgehog wielding her massive Piko Piko Hammer for heavy ground slams, spin jumps, and powerful area-of-effect attacks.

ROUGE

Cunning treasure hunter with aerial mobility, bomb drops, and swift kick combos designed to bypass obstacles and disarm foes.

CREAM

Flies gracefully using her large ears and commands Cheese the Chao for long-range homing strikes and defensive support.

MIGHTY

Durable wanderer with a heavy shell that deflects incoming hazards, spikes, and enemy projectiles with ease.

MIKU

Virtual idol using rhythm-based music waves and sound attacks to stun enemies and boost movement speed across the stage.

TIMMY

Accompanied by Cosmo & Wanda, summoning magical wishes for wild, unpredictable gameplay transformations and stage clearing effects.

LINCOLN

Master strategist who uses comic book hero tactics and family gadgets to improvise solutions to difficult platforming traps.

LOLA

Pageant powerhouse whose fierce attitude, royal tantrums, and swift car dashes overpower obstacles in her path.

THE UNHOLY ALLIANCE

ROBOTNIK

The mad genius combining dark technology with interdimensional machines to rewrite reality and rule all worlds.

PLANKTON

Mastermind behind micro-drones and giant mech suits, seeking total dimensional dominance and secret formula secrets.

MOJO JOJO

Super-intelligent primate wielding Chemical X powered superweapons and elaborate evil schemes to eliminate the heroes.
