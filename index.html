MAIN PLAYABLE CHARACTERS

Sonic the Hedgehog

The Blue Blur who runs at supersonic speeds, ready to lead the team against dimensional chaos.

Miles “Tails” Prower

Sonic's brilliant sidekick who uses his twin tails to fly and invents tech to save the day.

Knuckles the Echidna

The hot-tempered Master Emerald guardian equipped with legendary punching power.

Shadow the Hedgehog

The Ultimate Life Form utilizing Chaos Control to slice through dimensional enemies.

SpongeBob SquarePants

Bikini Bottom's favorite fry cook, armed with bubble-spinning powers and endless optimism.

Patrick Star

SpongeBob's loyal best friend who brings heavy-hitting, unpredictable star power.

Reluctant hero armed with sonic clarinet blasts and surprising defensive reach.

Goo Lagoon's muscular lifeguard who delivers raw physical strength and crushing attacks.

The toughest fighter of the trio, charging headfirst into battle with relentless brawling skills.
