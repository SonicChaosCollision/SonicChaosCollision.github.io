Sonic the Hedgehog: Chaos Collision

A Non-Profit Fan Game Experience

Overview

Welcome to the project page for my upcoming Sonic fan game. Inspired by frameworks like OpenMania and historical releases, this project aims to blend classic speed with entirely new level layouts, gimmicks, and mechanics.

Game Modes

Classic Mode

Experience a faithful recreation of beloved zones built from scratch with adjusted physics, custom sprite enhancements, and alternative pathways designed to test your platforming skills.

Challenge Mode

Ready for a real test? Tackle unique challenge acts featuring twisted layout variants, fast-paced gauntlets, and abstract stage obstacles designed for veterans.

The Dimensional Collision

Three worlds. One adventure.

A strange dimensional disturbance has thrown the worlds of Sonic the Hedgehog, SpongeBob SquarePants, and The Powerpuff Girls into a chaotic collision! As familiar dimensions fuse together, unique teams must join forces to uncover the source of the anomaly.

With Dr. Eggman, Plankton, and Mojo Jojo aligning into an ultimate villainous alliance, the stakes have never been higher. Race across retro-rendered 16-bit zones to collect the Chaos Emeralds, Secret Formula, and Chemical X samples before they fall into enemy hands!
