Sonic Megamix Mania is a fan-made modification for Sonic Mania Plus that aims to carry the torch of the discontinued ROM hack known as "Sonic the Hedgehog Megamix".

THE STORY

5 months have passed since Sonic the Hedgehog's previous plight against the nefarious Dr. Eggman. Ever since their first conflict the two sides have been battling constantly, with victory being just out of Eggman's grasp.

However, after years of fighting, Eggman had vanished after the incident on Space Colony ARK, leaving the world safely in Sonic's hands... or so it was thought.

PLAYABLE CHARACTERS

Sonic

Carefree and relaxed, but will never tolerate injustice. He is always running all over the world at supersonic speeds.

Tails

Sonic's faithful sidekick. Uses his two tails to fly like a helicopter and has a brilliant knack for machinery.

Knuckles

The hot-tempered guardian of the Master Emerald on Angel Island, equipped with fierce, powerful fists.

Shadow

The ultimate life form. Reawakened without any recollection of his past, driven by pure intuition and vengeance.

FEATURED ZONES

Sunny Shores

Dash through soft sand and lush grass in this ideal vacation spot turned battleground!

Dark Fortress

A tranquil, mysterious, yet dangerous castle lost in time. Beware of ancient traps guarding its secrets.

CLASSIC MODE

Re-experience the ROM hack that started it all! Play through faithful remakes of original zones complete with 3 acts each.

CHALLENGE MODE

13 unique challenge acts featuring twisted layouts, fast-paced gauntlets, and abstract obstacles.
