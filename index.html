THE COLLISION STORY

A massive, catastrophic dimensional rift has ripped across space-time, throwing the universes of SONIC THE HEDGEHOG, SPONGEBOB SQUAREPANTS, and THE POWERPUFF GIRLS into an unprecedented chaotic collision! As familiar landscapes fuse together—turning lush green hills into underwater trenchways and suburban skies into mechanical battle zones—the very fabric of reality begins to unravel.

Across these shattered dimensions, unlikely teams are forced to adapt. When the CHAOTIX pick up a high-paying lead on a string of reality-warping glitches, Vector, Espio, and Charmy begin tracking a trail of anomalies across the merging zones. Deep underwater, SANDY CHEEKS detects a massive spike in localized pressure and energy, diving straight into the rift to trace its source. Meanwhile, in Townsville, PROFESSOR UTONIUM analyzes a wave of unnatural energy signatures in his lab, discovering that their universe is actively merging with two others. Equipping BLOSSOM, BUBBLES, and BUTTERCUP with his findings, he sends them into the sky to head off the threat.

As these separate investigations converge, the heroes realize they aren't the only ones exploiting the rift. DR. EGGMAN, PLANKTON, and MOJO JOJO have formed a dangerous, ego-fueled alliance. By combining Eggman’s robotic engineering, Plankton’s cunning miniature tech, and Mojo Jojo’s mutated intellect, the trio seeks to harness the collision to construct the ultimate interdimensional empire.

To stop them and restore balance to their worlds, Sonic and his allies must embark on a high-speed journey across twisted, hybrid zones. But saving reality won't be easy—they'll need to locate and secure the CHAOS EMERALDS, the SECRET FORMULA, and CHEMICAL X before the villainous alliance uses their combined power to rewrite existence forever!

The collision has begun. Multi-dimensional leads are aligning. Three worlds. One ultimate adventure.

SONIC

Sonic is a blue hedgehog who lives on planet Earth. He is always running all over the world at supersonic speeds, hence his name. He is carefree and relaxed but will never tolerate injustice, doing anything he can to help those in need. He also has a quick wit, a short temper, and a fairly cocky attitude.

TAILS

Miles, better known by his nickname "Tails", is Sonic's faithful sidekick. Despite being bullied for having two tails, he is able to use them to fly like a helicopter. Miles has a knack for machinery and has grown into a confident young fox thanks to Sonic!

KNUCKLES

Named after his fierce fists that define his fighting style, Knuckles the Echidna is a hot-tempered inhabitant of Angel Island, the last guardian of the Master Emerald. As the last of his race, he bears the weight of a duty that often keeps him isolated from the world below.

SHADOW

Shadow is the ''ultimate life form'', created in an experiment by Professor Gerald Robotnik aboard the Space Colony ARK. His exact age isn't known, but he is assumed to be around 50 years old. Once hungry for revenge after the death of Maria, Gerald's granddaughter, he was able to remember his promise to her that he would protect humanity and put his life on the line to do so. Shadow was barely able to survive his fall into Earth’s atmosphere by preserving some energy through his secondary inhibitor ring. His memories would be lost however.

SPONGEBOB

SpongeBob is an endlessly optimistic sea sponge living in a pineapple under the sea in Bikini Bottom. Working as a fry cook at the Krusty Krab, his childlike wonder and boundless energy often lead him into bizarre situations alongside his best friend, Patrick. Equipped with his trusty Jellyfish Net, giant red Karate Gloves, and an indestructible buoyant body, SpongeBob approaches every new world with pure excitement and unyielding loyalty to his crew.

PATRICK

Patrick is a blissful, easygoing pink starfish whose lack of common sense is matched only by his deep devotion to his best friend, SpongeBob. Spending most of his time sleeping under a rock, Patrick’s surprisingly massive physical strength and complete immunity to panic make him an unpredictable force. Whether he is swinging a net or charging headfirst into confusion, Patrick somehow stumbles his way through high-stakes situations unscathed.

SQUIDWARD

Squidward is a cynical, self-proclaimed artist and clarinet player who wants nothing more than peace, quiet, and recognition for his talents. Unluckily for him, he is constantly dragged out of his home by SpongeBob and Patrick's absurd adventures. Though he maintains a miserable attitude and complains through every step of the journey, his stubborn frustration often translates into surprisingly effective defensive moves when pushed too far.

LARRY

Larry is Bikini Bottom’s resident beach heartthrob, bodybuilder, and head lifeguard at Goo Lagoon. Obsessed with fitness, personal health, and "living large," Larry approaches every challenge as a chance to flex his physique and inspire others. His massive claws, high jump height, and raw power make him a solid powerhouse on the team, always ready to lend a hand to anyone looking to hit the gym or save the day.

BLOSSOM

Created in a laboratory from sugar, spice, everything nice, and Chemical X, Blossom is the self-appointed leader of the Powerpuff Girls. Recognized by her signature giant red hair bow, she possesses an advanced intellect, tactical foresight, and a rare Ice Breath ability. Blossom keeps her sisters focused during battle, weighing options carefully before leading the charge against monsters and mad scientists alike.

BUBBLES

Bubbles is the sweet, sensitive soul of the Powerpuff Girls, capable of speaking multiple languages and communicating with animals. While she prefers playing with her stuffed bear, Octi, and drawing colorful pictures, pushing her too far unleashes a fierce inner strength. Her Sonic Scream can shatter steel, proving to villains everywhere that her gentle nature should never be mistaken for weakness.

BUTTERCUP

Buttercup is the ultimate tomboy and muscle of the Powerpuff Girls. She despises planning, rules, and talking things out, preferring to hit first and ask questions later. Floating into battle with a heavy scowl and green energy trail, Buttercup uses relentless physical strength, fast punches, and aggressive rushes to smash straight through enemy defenses.

AMY

Amy Rose, formerly known as “Rosy the Rascal” is a pink hedgehog with a strong admiration for Sonic. Amy loves fashion, performing tarot card readings, and helping those in need. After being rescued by Sonic from Metal Sonic on Little Planet, she uses her Piko Piko Hammer to defend herself and assist her friends. Her compassion is what allowed Shadow to briefly set aside his quest for revenge and continues to inspire others across her adventures. Feeling nostalgic, she decided to wear an old favorite outfit of hers as she travels along with Sonic on his new adventure.

MIGHTY

Mighty is a red and black armadillo with a strong sense of justice. Despite being a vagabond, he has recently decided to assist Charmy, Vector, and Espio in forming the Chaotix detective agency for the time being. His thirst for adventure allows him to be in tune with the natural elements of Water, Fire, and Electricity. Thanks to his shell, Mighty is extremely durable, able to block any incoming projectiles. Just like his name implies, he's remarkably strong!

RISTAR

A spirited cosmic hero from the Valdi System, Ristar is a shooting star driven by a powerful sense of justice and a desire to protect the galaxy. Equipped with incredible elastic arms, he grips onto opponents, swings off environment hazards, and flings himself through the air with comet-like speed. Beneath his cheerful and bright exterior, Ristar possesses unstoppable determination, using his stretchable strikes and fearless orbital attacks to face down any cosmic threat that endangers the multiverse.

HATSUNE MIKU

Hatsune Miku is a global digital idol defined by her iconic turquoise twintails and cybernetic pop aesthetic. Performing across digital dimensions, she uses the power of rhythm, light waves, and musical energy to boost her allies' speed and morale. Her bright presence and high-octane agility bring a unique, fast-paced rhythm to the team's ongoing journey.

TIMMY

Timmy is an ordinary ten-year-old kid who nobody understands, granted Fairy Godparents named Cosmo and Wanda to make his wishes come true. Though his impulsive, poorly thought-out wishes usually create chaotic disasters, Timmy is an expert at thinking on his feet to fix his mistakes. Equipped with magical tools and chaotic reality-warping gear, he maneuvers through danger with unpredictable luck.

LINCOLN

As the middle child and only boy in a household of ten sisters, Lincoln Loud is an expert in crowd control, crisis management, and complex planning. A massive fan of comic books and video games, he uses his "battle plans" to organize his team through chaotic environments. Lincoln relies on quick wits, tactical setups, and teamwork to outsmart opponents far stronger than himself.

LOLA

Don't let the pink dress and tiara fool you—Lola is a fierce, ruthless competitor who stops at nothing to win. Driven by her ambition to claim the ultimate multiverse trophy, she uses her speed, pageant accessories, and unpredictable tantrums to overpower opponents. Behind her bratty exterior lies a surprisingly sharp combatant who fights dirty to protect her crown and outshine the rest of the roster.

DR. ROBOTNIK

Dr. Ivo "Eggman" Robotnik is a brilliant scientist with a claimed IQ of 300, driven by an overwhelming desire to build his dream utopia, Eggmanland. Utilizing vast armies of Badniks, colossal battle mechs, and ancient power sources like the Chaos Emeralds, he constantly devises elaborate schemes for world domination. Despite his theatrical ego and constant defeats at the hands of Sonic, his engineering genius makes him an ever-present threat.

PLANKTON

Sheldon J. Plankton is a tiny, single-eyed micro-organism with a massive god complex. Operating from his failing restaurant, the Chum Bucket, Plankton spends his life devising elaborate schemes to steal the Krabby Patty Secret Formula. Utilizing giant laser cannons, mechanical suits, and mind-control devices, his small stature hides an aggressive mastermind who will gladly team up with evil forces if it gets him closer to absolute power.

MOJO JOJO

Once a normal lab assistant monkey named Jojo, an explosion involving Chemical X mutated his brain, giving him super-intelligence, a towering skull, and a hatred for humanity. Speaking in long, overly detailed monologues, Mojo Jojo operates out of his volcano observatory, constantly building giant death-ray robots to defeat the Powerpuff Girls and rule the world.
