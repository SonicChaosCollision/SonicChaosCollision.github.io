The Dimensional Collision

Three worlds. One adventure.

A strange dimensional disturbance has thrown the worlds of Sonic the Hedgehog, SpongeBob SquarePants, and The Powerpuff Girls into a chaotic collision! As familiar dimensions fuse together, unique teams must join forces to uncover the source of the anomaly.

With Dr. Eggman, Plankton, and Mojo Jojo aligning into an ultimate villainous alliance, the stakes have never been higher. Race across retro-rendered 16-bit Sega Genesis/32X styled zones to collect the Chaos Emeralds, Secret Formula, and Chemical X samples before they fall into enemy hands!
