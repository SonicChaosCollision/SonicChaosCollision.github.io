Main Playable Characters

Sonic the Hedgehog

The fastest thing alive. Leading the charge across colliding dimensions to rally his allies and restore the broken timelines before reality completely shatters.

Miles "Tails" Prower

Providing the crucial mechanical genius and tracking equipment needed to analyze the massive cross-universal dimensional rifts tearing through space.

Knuckles the Echidna

Using his raw power and experience as the Master Emerald's guardian to shatter the structural fortresses of the newly formed alliance of evil.

Shadow the Hedgehog

Operating solo from the dark depths of the rifts. He is determined to eliminate Dr. Eggman's new multidimensional accomplices by any means necessary.

SpongeBob SquarePants

Armed with a high-powered bubble wand and pure, unshakeable optimism. He steps out of his comfort zone to rescue Bikini Bottom from Plankton's grandest scheme.

Patrick Star

Bringing unpredictable physical strength, profound confusion, and absolute loyalty to his best buddy as they march through unfamiliar worlds.

Squidward Tentacles

Reluctantly joining the heroic resistance solely because the universal collision ruined his peaceful afternoon. He wants his quiet neighborhood back.

Larry the Lobster

Bringing elite athleticism and heavy muscle straight from the shores of Goo Lagoon to act as a frontline defensive wall for the team.

Blossom Utonium

The brilliant tactical commander of the Powerpuff Girls. She works alongside Tails to coordinate precise cross-world strategies against Mojo Jojo.

Bubbles Utonium

Spreading joy, laughter, and hardcore power. Her devastating sonic screams and ability to talk to local creatures prove invaluable across the dimensions.

Buttercup Utonium

Townsville's toughest fighter is incredibly eager to throw hands with Mojo's new mechanical allies. She charges headfirst into any fight.
