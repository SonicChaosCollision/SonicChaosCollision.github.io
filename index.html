Sonic the Hedgehog: Chaos Collision

A Harmony Framework Non-Profit Retro Project

The Dimensional Collision

Three worlds. One adventure.

A strange dimensional disturbance has thrown the worlds of Sonic the Hedgehog, SpongeBob SquarePants, and The Powerpuff Girls into a chaotic collision! As familiar dimensions fuse together, unique teams must join forces to uncover the source of the anomaly.

With Dr. Eggman, Plankton, and Mojo Jojo aligning into an ultimate villainous alliance, the stakes have never been higher. Race across retro-rendered 16-bit Sega Genesis/32X styled zones to collect the Chaos Emeralds, Secret Formula, and Chemical X samples before they fall into enemy hands!

Main Playable Characters

Sonic

Armed with his Super Peel Out and Jump Dash, Sonic races into the dimensional rift to grab the Chaos Emeralds first.

Tails

Utilizing his flight and mechanical tools, Tails builds gadgets to track the origins of the strange world collision.

Knuckles

Breaking open walls with his fists, Knuckles tracks down missing fragments of the Master Emerald across new landscapes.

Shadow

Harnessing Chaos energy and dual thrusters, Shadow tracks Eggman and his new sinister allies with ruthless intent.

SpongeBob

Equipped with bubble-bounce attacks and quick karate chops, SpongeBob steps out of the sea to rescue the Secret Formula.

Patrick

Providing pure physical muscle to the team, Patrick can lift and hurl heavy level blocks directly into robotic enemies.

Squidward

Attacking using powerful sound waves from his clarinet, Squidward fights solely to fix his universe and get his quiet back.

Larry

Charging straight through hazardous traps with armored beach-guard tackles, this powerhouse clears paths forward.

Blossom

Leading the Townsville heroes with careful analytical flight and ice breath, Blossom tracks the combined villain grid.

Bubbles

Unleashing massive sonic scream blasts and high-speed flight maneuvers, Bubbles moves through layout dangers with joy.

Buttercup

Operating as the ultimate team brawler, Buttercup uses close-quarters energy punches to smash boss mechanics instantly.
